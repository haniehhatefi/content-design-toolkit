# Contributing

This repository is a personal portfolio project. It documents a content design toolkit
developed as a structured demonstration of content design practice, AI-assisted content
workflows, and quality evaluation methods.

---

## Status

This is not an actively maintained open-source project. The toolkit reflects decisions made
during its development, and the content is not under continuous revision.

Pull requests are not part of a formal contribution process. The maintainer may review
suggestions at their discretion, but there is no commitment to respond to or merge
external changes.

---

## Feedback and corrections

Feedback is welcome. If you notice an error, a broken link, or an inconsistency, you can:

- Open a GitHub issue describing the problem and where it appears
- Contact the maintainer directly via the details on their portfolio

Factual corrections and broken links are more likely to be acted on than requests to
restructure the toolkit or add new content.

---

## Repository structure and conventions

If you are reading this repository in detail, the following may be useful.

**Directory structure**

The toolkit is organised into six numbered directories. Each contains a README describing
its contents and how it fits within the toolkit.

**Guidance levels**

Documents in `02-ux-writing-standards/` label each piece of guidance as one of three
levels: Rule, Recommendation, or Contextual. These labels are explained in that module's
README.

**Illustrative outputs**

Documents in `05-practical-experiments/` use outputs constructed to demonstrate specific
aspects of the toolkit's methods. These are not outputs captured from actual model runs,
and they are labelled explicitly throughout.

**Writing style**

The toolkit follows its own standards. Documents aim for clear, direct writing, avoid
passive constructions where active ones are clearer, and follow the voice attributes
described in `01-foundations/voice-principles.md`.
